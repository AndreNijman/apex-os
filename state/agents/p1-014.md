## The plan, decided

**P1-014.** The environment is the provider's knowledge and the approval is the
framework's, so the two halves are split at `Bound`:

1. `Bound` gains `approval: Approval` — `Standing` (the grant is the whole
   decision) or `Required(why)`. This closes half of the "Bound carries no
   provider payload" gap **generally**, not for Cloudflare.
2. A store table of **one-shot owner approvals**, a protocol verb, and
   consumption inside `use_capability` *after* the pin and the `creates` check
   and *before* the value is read. Spent on commit, not on success — refunding
   a failed perform would let an agent probe. Guarded by a mutex on `Service`,
   because of the threading note above.
3. `binding.rs` reads `unattended = true` under `[cloudflare.<env>]`. Default
   polarity, §13.8: `preview` and `staging` unattended, **everything else
   protected** — not "production is special-cased by name", so `prod`, `live`
   and `production-eu` do not get a free pass by being spelled differently.
4. Gated: the operations that change what a protected worker **serves** —
   `worker.deploy`, `worker.rollback`, `wrangler.deploy`. NOT
   `worker.upload-version`: §13.7 puts the upload *before* the approval point,
   and gating it would force approval at step one of a five-step flow.
5. `approval_policy` gains two distinct words: `approval-required` on the
   refusal, `owner` when one is spent.
